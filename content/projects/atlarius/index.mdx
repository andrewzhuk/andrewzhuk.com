## A more connected way to work

Technical work spreads across tools, conversations, and machines. Reconstructing that context takes time and attention that could go into the work itself.

I’m building Atlarius as a native workspace for teams: a place to bring tools and conversations together, with more control for the people doing the work.

## My role

As founder and CEO, I work across product direction, software architecture, and hands-on engineering. Atlarius brings together my experience with applications, backend systems, and infrastructure.

## The direction

The starting point is a native Mac application for technical teams. Connected tools, team communication, local-first architecture, and extensibility shape the product and engineering decisions.

The image above shows an interface preview using a demo workspace. Atlarius is in development; it is an ongoing product rather than a finished case study.
